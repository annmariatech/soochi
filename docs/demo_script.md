# Soochi — Every Scrap Has a Second Story

**0–15 seconds: The problem**

Every day, useful fabric scraps are thrown away because people don't know how to reuse them. At the same time, someone else may need exactly that amount of fabric for a small product.

**15–30 seconds: Our solution**

Soochi connects leftover fabric with creative possibilities. Users can record their fabric scraps, including their dimensions and material, and explore products they could make.

**30–50 seconds: How it works**

In Scraps to Products mode, users select their available scraps and explore suitable product ideas. In Products to Scraps mode, users choose a product and its required dimensions to find compatible fabric.

**50–65 seconds: Community sharing**

If a user doesn't have enough fabric, Soochi can help identify missing material and find compatible scraps offered by other users, depending on available community data.

**65–80 seconds: Impact**

Our goal is to reduce textile waste, encourage creative reuse, and make sustainable crafting easier for students, home sewers, and small creators.

**80–90 seconds: Closing**

Soochi gives leftover fabric a new purpose. Because every scrap has a second story.
